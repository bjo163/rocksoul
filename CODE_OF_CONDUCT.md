# Code of Conduct

RockSoul welcomes technical disagreement, critical review, and careful challenge of ideas. Contributions should remain respectful, specific, evidence-oriented, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- provide reproducible evidence when making technical claims;
- respect privacy and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a review or recommendation.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive or unsafe content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

Security disclosures must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) rather than being posted publicly.
